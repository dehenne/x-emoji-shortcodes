# Privacy Policy

X Emoji Shortcodes does not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share personal data.

The extension runs locally in your browser on supported X/Twitter pages and only replaces recognized emoji shortcodes inside editable text fields. It does not call external APIs, does not use analytics, and does not require extension permissions beyond content script access to supported pages.

## Data processed locally

The extension reads the text around your current cursor position only to detect patterns like `:party:`. Replacement happens locally in the page. No typed content leaves your browser because of this extension.

## Contact

For privacy or security questions, open an issue at https://github.com/dehenne/x-emoji-shortcodes/issues.
